How a case flows

Four steps, one surgeon in command of every one of them.

Dictate or type

On-device speech — the audio never leaves the Mac. Procedure recognized across 50 built-in procedures.

Review the structured note

Kestrel builds the structured op note from your dictation. Ambiguous field or laterality? It stops and asks — it will not invent a detail to finish.

Decide the codes

CPT suggestions with modifier logic and laterality/bundling warnings. Where a human must decide, it says so.

Sign, export, communicate

Encrypted case store, version history, tamper-evident audit trail of every export. Patient instruction sheets and consent explainers in English or Spanish.

Local-only is the architecture, not a setting

There is no cloud to trust, no server to breach, and no account to create. This is enforced in code at the validation seam — the only configurable endpoint is your own machine's loopback, and every other host is refused.

Encrypted at rest

Every case is AES-GCM sealed; the key lives in your Mac's Keychain. Filenames carry no patient identifiers.

Crash-safe by test

Kill the app mid-dictation, relaunch — nothing is lost. That behavior is pinned by an automated test suite, not a slogan.

Audited exits

Every export and clipboard copy lands in a local hash-chained audit trail. Teaching copies are de-identified and risk-graded.
